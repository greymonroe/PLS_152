Instructor: Grey Monroe (gmonroe@ucdavis.edu) · TA: Alice Pierce

Meetings: Tuesdays & Thursdays, 3:10–5:00 PM, Veihmeyer Hall 212 — one continuous block with a short break (the registrar’s separate 20-min “discussion” listing is an artifact; plan for the full 3:10–5:00).
